# Vision

**Status: living.** Rewritten when the goal changes, not on a schedule.
Carries no measurements; those live with the work that produced them.

Both sections are filled from the brief at initialisation and live on from
there. Diverging from the brief is not an error: the brief stays the assignment
as it was set, and this document says where the project has got to.

Git keeps the history of edits; there is no changelog here. If the question
narrowed or the boundary moved after the first numbers existed, that is a
decision, and it is recorded as one, with a date.
